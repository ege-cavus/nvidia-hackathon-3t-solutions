# Deployment guidance

Illustrative guidance for demo use. Replace with force policy.

## Patrol deployment
- Minor incidents: 2 patrol cars.
- Severity 3: 4 patrol cars (scene, cordons, witnesses, traffic).
- Severity 4+: 6 patrol cars plus a drone for overhead imagery; consider Gold/Silver/Bronze command.
- Severity 5: request the mobile command unit.

## Firearms incidents
- Minimum 2 armed response vehicles (ARVs); target arrival 15 minutes.
- Unarmed officers hold the outer cordon only. Inner cordon 100 m, outer cordon 300 m until the threat is contained.
- The scene is not safe for other agencies until declared safe by the Tactical Firearms Commander.
- Request a dog unit for searches.

## Public disorder
- 2 public order vans (PSU) minimum; escalate via Silver.

## Road traffic collisions
- 1 roads policing unit for collision investigation; fatal or life-changing injuries require full road closure and a forensic collision investigator.

# Cordons and scene management

## Outer cordon
Police set and hold the outer cordon. It must enclose any evacuation zone set by fire & rescue, and the RVPs of all agencies should sit just outside it.

## Crime scene preservation
Log everyone entering the inner cordon. Agree a common approach path with fire and ambulance. Preservation of life always takes precedence over preservation of evidence.

## Road closures and traffic management
Close roads serving the incident as requested by the lead agency. Always keep a blue-light route for ambulance access and egress; tell ambulance control which route is open.

## Joint working (JESIP)
Co-locate commanders, communicate in plain language, coordinate, jointly understand risk and share situational awareness. One multi-agency RVP is preferred.

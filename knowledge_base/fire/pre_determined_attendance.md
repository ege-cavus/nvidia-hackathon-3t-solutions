# Pre-determined attendance (PDA)

Illustrative guidance for demo use. Replace with the service's approved PDA tables.

## Dwelling fire
- 2 fire engines (pumps). Add 1 pump if persons reported.
- Severity 4 or more, or fire spread beyond room of origin: 4 pumps and request an incident commander at Level 2.
- Max acceptable first-pump arrival: 8 minutes; second pump 10 minutes.

## High-rise residential fire
- Minimum 4 pumps plus 1 aerial ladder platform (ALP), regardless of initial reports.
- Bridgehead established two floors below the fire floor. Evacuation strategy (stay put vs simultaneous) decided by the incident commander and reviewed continuously.
- Evacuation radius for falling debris: at least 150 m from the base of the building on affected faces.
- Inner cordon at least 100 m; only BA-equipped crews inside.

## Road traffic collision with persons trapped
- 2 pumps plus 1 rescue tender (hydraulic rescue equipment).
- Rescue tender arrival target 15 minutes; beyond that, consider the nearest pump with cutting equipment.
- Coordinate extrication plan with the ambulance clinician (time-critical vs controlled release).

## Hazardous materials and gas leaks
- 2 pumps plus hazmat unit. Approach from upwind and uphill.
- Initial inner cordon 200 m for unknown substances; evacuation radius 400 m until substance identified.
- Decontamination at the inner cordon; no casualties leave the scene without decontamination.

## Flooding and water rescue
- Water rescue unit plus 1 pump. Only water-rescue trained crews enter moving water.

# Scene safety and cordons

## Inner cordon and entry control
The inner cordon encloses the hazard area. Fire & rescue control entry: all personnel entering must be logged at the entry control point and wear appropriate PPE (BA for fire and smoke). Scene entry is "prohibited" for other agencies while there is active fire, collapse risk or an unidentified hazardous substance; it becomes "controlled" once the incident commander judges escorted entry safe.

## Casualty handover
Casualties rescued from the inner cordon are handed to ambulance crews at a casualty handover point on the cordon line. Ambulance HART teams are the only ambulance staff who may operate inside the inner cordon.

## Staging and rendezvous
Appliances stage at an upwind rendezvous point (RVP) outside the inner cordon. For multi-agency incidents, agree one RVP with police and ambulance.

## Road closures
Request police closure of the road directly serving the incident for pump positioning and hose lines. Keep one lane clear for ambulance access where possible.

## Collapse zone
For structures with fire damage beyond one floor, establish a collapse zone of 1.5 times the building height.

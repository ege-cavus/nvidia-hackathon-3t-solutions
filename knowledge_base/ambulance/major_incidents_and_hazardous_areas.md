# Major incidents and hazardous areas

## Declaring a major incident
Declare when the number of casualties exceeds what normal resources can manage, typically 5 or more P1/P2 casualties. Send a METHANE report: Major incident declared, Exact location, Type of incident, Hazards, Access and egress, Number of casualties, Emergency services present and required.

## Triage
Triage sieve on arrival, triage sort at the casualty clearing station. Record P1 (immediate), P2 (urgent), P3 (delayed), dead.

## HART (Hazardous Area Response Team)
Only HART operates inside an inner cordon, in hazardous materials, firearms (warm zone) or confined spaces. Request HART for any hazmat incident, firearms incident with casualties, collapse, or mass-casualty incident.

## Scene access
Ambulance crews need a clear route to a loading point as close as safely possible to the casualties. Request police to keep a blue-light route open through any road closure. Crews do not enter a scene that police or fire have declared unsafe; casualties are brought to the cordon instead.

## Firearms and stabbing incidents
Crews wait at the police RVP until police declare the scene safe. Expect to treat penetrating trauma; consider tourniquets and rapid conveyance to the major trauma centre.

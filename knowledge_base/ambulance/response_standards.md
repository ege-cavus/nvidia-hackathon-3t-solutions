# Response standards

Illustrative guidance for demo use. Replace with the trust's approved standards.

## Response categories
- Category 1 (life-threatening, e.g. cardiac arrest, not breathing): send a rapid response vehicle plus an emergency ambulance; mean response target 7 minutes.
- Category 2 (emergency, e.g. stroke, major trauma): emergency ambulance; mean response target 18 minutes.
- Category 3 and 4: urgent and non-urgent; may be handled by clinical triage.

## Casualty to ambulance ratio
- 1 emergency ambulance per P1 or P2 casualty for up to 4 casualties.
- Beyond 4 casualties, 1 additional ambulance per 2 further casualties, plus a casualty clearing station.

## Critical care
Send a critical care car for persons trapped, major trauma, or severity 4+. Target arrival 20 minutes.

## Receiving hospitals (Sheffield)
- Northern General Hospital: major trauma centre for adults. All major trauma and burns triage here.
- Royal Hallamshire Hospital: emergency department for non-major-trauma adults.
- Sheffield Children's Hospital: paediatric emergency department and paediatric trauma.

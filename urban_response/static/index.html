    About this data

    SURU runs a 999 call through Qwen (duplicate check and routing), three Nemotron agency agents in parallel (A1 police, A2 ambulance, A3 fire), and Nemotron CR, which settles conflicts and makes the final dispatch. Code enforces life-safety floors, crews every vehicle and re-checks CR's choices; Qwen then scores the outcome.

    Every decision is tagged with who made it: a model, rules, a fallback (the model call failed and rules filled in), a life-safety floor, or mock (no model server).

    Stations, unit counts, crews, coordinates and the agencies' knowledge base are approximate and illustrative. Basemap © OpenMapTiles, data © OpenStreetMap contributors.

    Simulated decisions for a hackathon demo. Not for operational use.
